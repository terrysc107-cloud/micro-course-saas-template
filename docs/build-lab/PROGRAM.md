# The Build Lab offer and delivery model

## Promise

Build an AI operating foundation you understand and can lead. By the end, an owner should be able to demonstrate an agent charter, verified business context, a CEO/board decision process, one recurring workflow, and an operating guide. Completion means demonstrating the work against reviewed criteria; it is not a promise of autonomous business management or guaranteed financial results.

## The four weeks

| Week | Shared capability | Evidence reviewed in class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Business context and agent identity | Agent accurately explains the business, identifies missing information, and follows defined authority limits |
| 2 | CEO and board roles | A real decision meeting with grounded recommendations, disagreements, and owner decisions |
| 3 | Memory and standing work | One recurring workflow uses corrected facts and previous decisions, and identifies missing inputs |
| 4 | Review and ownership | Owner demonstrates a run, catches a bad result, and recovers from a missing input |

Owners work with their own tools and businesses. They arrive at shared capabilities through different examples and preparation guides. API and coding experience change the setup path, not the completion standard.

## Offer ladder

1. My AI Board foundation course: self-paced entry (existing offer).
2. Your AI Operating Company: the four-week guided foundation lab.
3. Rotating advanced labs: content engine, follow-up system, business front door, operations brief.
4. A future premium implementation engagement, if Terry chooses to offer it, can include individual setup and more hands-on integration.

The old Build Lab run is preserved as a legacy offer for existing customers. Its materials can inform future advanced labs; the new product does not promise every old deliverable inside the foundation.

## Price recommendation

**Start by testing $1,995 for eight owners.** At this scope, $5,000 would need a more individual implementation/support promise and stronger proof of delivered outcomes. A beginner learning to run a useful foundation is a narrower promise than a new website plus content automation, CRM, accounting, and an autonomous business operator.

This is a pricing hypothesis, not a measured willingness-to-pay result. Do not advertise an invented higher comparison price. Validate it through qualified applications, accepted-to-paid conversion, reasons for declining, actual teaching time, completion, and demand for the next lab.

Illustrative arithmetic, before fees, software, refunds, acquisition, and labor:

- Eight owners × $1,995 = $15,960 gross cohort revenue.
- Four owners × $1,995 = $7,980 gross cohort revenue.
- Four two-hour sessions = 8 live hours, if that duration is confirmed.
- Eight owners × 30 minutes preparation = 4 hours.
- Eight owners × 20 minutes feedback × four weeks = about 10.7 hours.
- Add an illustrative four hours of cohort administration: about 26.7 hours total before marketing and unexpected support.

These are planning assumptions. Track the first cohort's actual time before increasing capacity or shortening the rotation. Scarcity comes from review capacity and scheduled sessions. An interest-list signup does not reserve a seat. The database enforces paid-plus-held capacity; the page does not fabricate seats remaining.

## Before the first session

Application: owner, business, offer/customer, recurring bottleneck, desired outcome, AI experience, weekly practice time.

After enrollment: customers/team/journey/baseline; website/hosting/payments/email/calendar/CRM/automation/access; API/code/device/learning needs/software budget; values/voice/authority/source documents/first workflow/exclusions.

Terry reviews each owner before teaching. The reviewed plan identifies assumptions, missing access, a small first workflow, preparation steps, acceptance criteria, and a guide they can reuse. AI-assisted preparation is optional, and every plan needs instructor review.

## Live session format to confirm

A useful working structure is a short teaching block, a demonstration, owner work time, and deliverable reviews. Group work should use sanitized information. Review is an adjustment loop: demonstrate → identify the gap → change the system → demonstrate again. The site promises four working sessions; exact duration, dates, and between-session response times must be confirmed before checkout opens.

## Deliberate first-release boundaries

- One first workflow per owner. No blanket promise to connect every business tool.
- Software/API/hosting costs are separate and discussed before connecting accounts.
- No automatic external agent actions or customer messaging are performed by this learning platform.
- No credentials or customer/patient records in intake; document types and sanitized examples only.
- Human review before personalized plans reach students.
- Workspace updates are built. Automated application/plan email notifications and cohort-calendar invitations still need the connected delivery setup if desired.
- New cohorts are configured through the database by an administrator. Advanced curricula must be prepared before those interest lists become paid offers.
