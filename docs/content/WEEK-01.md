## Day 6 (Sat) — Short video or text — one tactic

**Topic:** Permission modes, the thing most people get wrong on day one.

> Read your status bar before you type anything.
>
> `⏸ manual mode on` means every action comes to you.
> `⏵⏵ auto mode on` means a classifier is reviewing actions instead of you.
>
> Those are very different sessions. Which one you get depends on your plan and
> your installed version. Shift+Tab moves between them.
