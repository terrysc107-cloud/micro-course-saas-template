# Decisions

> Newest at the top.
>
> **What would change my mind** is the valuable column: a decision with no
> reversal condition is just an opinion with a date on it.
>
> **Rank** records the authority the call was made under. Yours say `me`. Once
> a seat is deciding things it stamps its own rank, and that column is what
> makes a later promotion request checkable rather than a claim.
>
> Fill in **Outcome** later, honestly, including when you were wrong. A log with
> no bad outcomes in it is a log nobody is updating.
>
> Example of a filled row:
> `| Mar 03 | me | Stopped the $200 starter package | Same effort as the $800
> one | Three people ask unprompted in a month | |`
