## The Hidden Market for Custom Tools

Most developers chase consumer apps or competitive SaaS markets. But the highest-paying, least-competitive niche is right in front of them: custom internal software for businesses.

Every business over ~5 employees has processes being run in spreadsheets, email chains, and manual steps that a simple web app could automate. They know they need it. They do not have the developer relationships to get it built.

That is your market.

## What Internal Tools Look Like

- **Admin dashboards** — manage users, orders, inventory, support tickets from one place
- **Employee portals** — time tracking, expense submission, PTO requests, onboarding checklists
- **Operations dashboards** — real-time view of key business metrics, pipeline health
- **Client portals** — let clients submit requests, view project status, upload files
- **Approval workflows** — purchase orders, content approvals, compliance sign-offs
- **Data entry tools** — replace spreadsheets with a proper form + database

<Callout type="tip">When scoping an internal tool project, start by asking the client: "What is the single most painful manual process your team does every week?" Build a tool for that first. A tool that solves the most painful problem gets used immediately and enthusiastically — which leads to referrals and expansion work.</Callout>

## Example: Building an Admin Panel

```
> Build a simple admin panel at /admin for our e-commerce app.
  
  Pages needed:
  1. /admin/orders — list all orders, filter by status, mark as shipped
  2. /admin/customers — list customers, view order history, add notes
  3. /admin/products — list/edit/toggle products active/inactive
  
  Security: admin panel route should only be accessible to users 
  where role = 'admin' in user_profiles table.
  Add middleware check for this.
  
  Follow the same Next.js + Supabase patterns as the rest of the app.
```

Claude Code builds this in a few sessions. You charge for 2-3 days of work.

## The Business Case to Clients

Calculate the ROI before you pitch:

```
Your client's team manually processes 50 orders/day from a spreadsheet.
Each order takes 5 minutes to process manually.
5 minutes × 50 orders × 250 working days = 10,417 hours/year.
At $20/hour fully-loaded cost, that is $208,000/year of manual processing.

Your tool automates 80% of it:
Savings = $166,400/year.
Your fee: $8,000 to build + $500/month to maintain.
ROI in: 2 weeks.
```

No business owner argues with a 2-week ROI.

## Finding Internal Tool Clients

- **Your network first** — every business owner you know has manual processes
- **Local business associations** — Chambers of Commerce, industry groups
- **LinkedIn outreach** — message operations managers at mid-size companies
- **Your current clients** — if you built their website, ask what they do manually
- **Reddit** — r/smallbusiness, r/entrepreneur — people post their pain points

The pitch: "I build custom software tools that automate your manual business processes. First conversation is free — I find the processes in your business that are costing you the most time and give you an honest assessment of what could be automated and what it would cost."

## Project Structure for Internal Tools

```
> I am building an employee expense management tool for a 20-person company.
  
  Requirements:
  - Employees submit expense reports with receipt photos
  - Manager approves or rejects each line item
  - Finance exports approved expenses to CSV for payroll
  - Admin can see all expenses by department and date range
  
  Design the database schema first, then we will build role by role:
  employee → manager → finance → admin
```

Building role by role lets you deliver and demo each piece, building client confidence.

<Callout type="success">Internal tools clients are the most likely to become recurring revenue — once they rely on a tool you built, they pay monthly for maintenance, ask for new features, and refer you to other businesses. One internal tools client can generate more revenue over 2 years than 10 one-off freelance projects.</Callout>

## Pricing Internal Tools

| Tool complexity | Price range |
|----------------|-------------|
| Simple (1 CRUD view + auth) | $1,500-3,000 |
| Medium (3-5 views, integrations) | $4,000-8,000 |
| Complex (10+ views, workflows, reporting) | $10,000-25,000 |
| Ongoing maintenance retainer | $200-500/month |

These prices feel high to developers who are used to hourly rates. To businesses, they are nothing compared to the problem being solved.
